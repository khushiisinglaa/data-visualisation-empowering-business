# Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

Virtual job simulation completed on Forage (Tata). I worked as a consultant for an online retail store and shared insights with the CEO and CMO.

## What I did

**1. Drafted questions for the CEO and CMO**
I wrote 8 questions, 4 for each leader. The CEO questions focused on revenue by region, monthly trends, seasonality, and top customers. The CMO questions focused on repeat customers, time to reorder, and revenue from repeat buyers.

**2. Chose the right visuals**
I learned how to pick the best chart for each business question so the message is clear. I completed a quiz on this.

**3. Cleaned the data**
Before any analysis, I removed bad data:
- Rows with quantity below 1 (returns)
- Rows with unit price below $0 (input errors)

**4. Created four visuals**
- **Q1:** Monthly revenue trend for 2011 to show seasonal patterns (CEO)
- **Q2:** Top 10 countries by revenue and quantity sold, without the United Kingdom (CMO)
- **Q3:** Top 10 customers by revenue, sorted from highest to lowest (CMO)
- **Q4:** Demand by country on a single map view, without the United Kingdom, to support expansion planning (CEO)

**5. Presented my findings**
I wrote a script and recorded a video of about 5 minutes for the CEO and CMO. I explained:
- How I loaded and cleaned the data
- What each visual shows and why it matters to their questions
- Which countries have the best chance for expansion
- Why the business does not depend on only a few customers

## Key insights
- Revenue is affected by seasonality, with sales rising towards the end of the year
- Some countries outside the UK show strong demand and are good targets for expansion
- Revenue is spread across many customers, so the business is not too dependent on a few
- Some regions have very low sales and may be new growth opportunities
